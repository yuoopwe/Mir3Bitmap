# In-game guide

From the game's Help/Guide pages.

## Stats

Quick guide to every visible stat, how it’s used, and how elements interact.

### Overview

- **Level & Experience**: Level gates gear and skills; Experience fills the bar until you level up and grow stronger.
- **Weight & Carrying**: Body and bag Weight show how much you can haul; staying under the limit keeps movement and regen normal.

### Offense

- **DC**: Physical damage roll for melee/ranged weapons; higher DC raises both minimum and maximum hit values.
- **MC**: Magic damage roll for nature/arcane spells; boosts the low and high ends of MC-scaling abilities.
- **SC**: Spirit damage roll for spiritual/chi skills; increases damage range on SC-scaling strikes and spells.
- **Accuracy**: Raises your chance to land physical attacks on nimble foes and reduces misses against high Agility.
- **Attack Speed**: Shortens the delay between swings or shots; faster Attack Speed means more attacks over time.

### Defense

- **AC**: AC shaves off incoming physical melee damage; higher values make weapon hits hurt less.
- **MAC**: MAC reduces incoming spell damage; pairs with MR to soften hostile magic.
- **MR**: MR cuts how much magic gets through; stacking it helps against casters and bosses.
- **Agility & Evasion**: Agility lowers the chance you are hit by physical attacks, acting as evasion against enemy accuracy.

### Vitals & Recovery

- **Health**: Your life pool; dropping to zero knocks you out. More Health means more room to survive burst damage.
- **Mana**: Resource for casting and skills; higher Mana lets you sustain abilities longer before resting.
- **Comfort**: Comfort improves natural regeneration tick rate for Health and Mana, shortening downtime between fights.
- **Regeneration**: Flat Regeneration bonuses add to the amount recovered each tick; combine with Comfort for steadier recovery.

### Speed & Control

- **Movement Speed**: Dictates how quickly you travel and kite; faster Movement Speed helps reposition and dodge monster attacks.
- **Cooldown & Cast Speed**: Cooldown reduction reuses skills sooner, while Cast Speed shortens the wind-up on spells.

### Elements

- **Elemental Attack**: Fire, Ice, Lightning, Wind, Holy, Dark, and Phantom add extra elemental damage on top of your base hit.
- **Elemental Resistance**: Matching Elemental Resistance reduces damage taken from that element; stack the one enemies use most.
- **Advantage & Disadvantage**: An element with Advantage deals bonus damage to its opposing element, while Disadvantage deals reduced damage.
- **Elemental Affinity**: Affinity marks your favored element: it slightly boosts your damage with that element and weakens incoming hits of the same type.

### Monsters & Target Info

- **Monster Display**: Target frames show Level, Health, Attack Speed, and Movement Speed icons so you know how hard and how fast a monster hits.
- **Family & Affinity Tags**: Undead, Mortal, or Tamable tags hint at skill interactions and whether companions can tame the target.

## Currencies

Overview of money types, where to earn them, and what they buy.

### Core

- **Gold & Silver**: Gold is the main trade currency; earn from loot or sales and spend on repairs, shops, and trading.
- **Trade Value**: NPCs use Value to price items; selling gear with higher value yields more coins back.

### Limited

- **Tokens & Vouchers**: Daily or event Tokens buy cosmetics, boosts, or unlocks; many are character-bound.
- **Dungeon Passes**: Instance tickets or Keys gate special bosses or chests; check expiry before saving them.

### Premium

- **Gems**: Premium Gems buy convenience items, cosmetics, or services; some purchases remain account-wide.
- **Conversion**: Watch rates when exchanging gems to gold; trading limits may cap daily Exchange amounts.

## Groups

How to form parties, share rewards, and coordinate with teammates.

### Setup

- **Creating**: Invite friends to form a Party; set loot rules before entering dungeons or bosses.
- **Roles**: Assign Tank, Damage, and Support so everyone knows their job in fights.

### Rewards

- **Loot Sharing**: Use round-robin, need/greed, or leader assign; confirm Binding rules before rolling.
- **XP & Credits**: Party members within range share Experience and quest credit; stay nearby to receive rewards.

## Guilds

Form long-term communities, unlock perks, and manage membership.

### Joining & Creating

- **Applications**: Apply to open guilds or accept invites; leaders can set Requirements like level or class.
- **Founding**: Create a guild with a name, emblem, and initial Funds; choose ranks and permissions early.

### Management

- **Ranks & Permissions**: Define ranks for Leadership, recruiters, and members; control storage access, invites, and buffs.
- **Perks**: Contribute gold or materials to unlock Buffs, vendor discounts, and shared utilities like ports.

### Activities

- **Events**: Schedule guild Events for bosses, sieges, or social nights; use calendar and alerts to gather.
- **Missions**: Complete guild quests or Contracts for reputation and currency that fund upgrades.

## Skills

Overview of learning, upgrading, and using character skills.

### Learning

- **Books & Trainers**: Acquire skill books or visit class trainers to unlock new abilities appropriate to your level.
- **Requirements**: Some skills need prerequisite abilities, stats, or quests—check the skill tooltip before learning.

### Upgrading

- **Rank Ups**: Consume books or materials to raise a skill’s rank, improving damage, duration, or utility.
- **Cooldown & Cost**: Higher ranks may change mana costs or cooldowns—balance upgrades with your resource pool.

### Usage

- **Hotkeys**: Place skills on your hotbar and bind keys for quick casting during combat.
- **Targeting**: Check whether a skill is single-target, area, or self-cast to aim it correctly in fights.

## Fishing

Guide to casting lines, catching fish, and using rewards.

### Getting Started

- **Gear Up**: Equip a fishing rod and bait before casting at a fishing spot or water tile.
- **Slots**: Place the rod in your main-hand/weapon slot and load bait into its consumable/ammo slot so each cast consumes a piece automatically.
- **Casting**: Face the water and cast your line; wait for a bite indicator before reeling in.

### Catching

- **Hook Timing**: Press the hook action when the float moves to secure the catch; early or late clicks can lose the fish.
- **Line Tension**: Manage tension while reeling; keep it in the safe zone to avoid line breaks and lost bait.

### Equipment Stats

- **Rod Stats**: Look for rods with higher Line Strength to resist breaks, Casting Speed to shorten throw time, and Tension Control to widen the safe reel window.
- **Bait & Accessories**: Bait with Bite Chance or Rarity modifiers draws more bites and rare finds; floats or lures that add Stability reduce tension spikes while reeling.

### Rewards

- **Fish Uses**: Cook, sell, or trade fish for quests; higher quality catches give better returns.
- **Treasure & Events**: Rare bites can yield treasure chests or event items—keep spare bait ready for streaks.

### Choosing a Class

- **Warrior**: A direct melee fighter built around sturdy equipment, weapon pressure and close-range control.
- **Wizard**: A ranged spellcaster with strong elemental attacks, area damage and careful positioning.
- **Taoist**: A flexible support fighter using spiritual magic, healing, summons and ranged pressure.
- **Assassin**: A fast close-range attacker using movement, timing and rapid weapon combinations.
- **Summoner**: A pet-focused caster who balances personal magic with summoned allies.
- **Druid**: A nature-focused class using forms, creatures, ailments and adaptable support.
- **Archer**: A mobile ranged weapon class using arrows, positioning and specialist ammunition.
- **Blade Dancer**: A mobile melee class using blades, rhythm and sustained offensive pressure.

### Combat Basics

- **Positioning**: Range, facing and movement matter. Keep an escape route, avoid standing inside dangerous effects and let sturdier allies take the first contact when grouping.
- **Controls**: Place frequently used skills and items on accessible hotkeys. The interface and key-binding options let you adjust the layout without changing your character's progression.
- **Death and recovery**: Death rules depend on the active realm and Hardcore choices. Check the character's active rules before entering unfamiliar or high-risk content.
