# Crafting recipes

Ingredients (9 slots), level and profession requirements.

## Engraving: Blank Rune

- Materials, level 0, profession 1, makes Blank Rune
- Needs: 3x Common Essence
- Unlock: Always known at Bloodline Grade F.
- Combines three Common Essences into one Blank Rune for the Essence Artisan.

## Farming: Copper Sprinkler

- Farming, level 0, profession 1, makes Copper Sprinkler
- Needs: 5x Copper Ore
- Unlock: Known at every crafting table. Gather the ore by mining.
- Place on a clear farm square. Automatically waters the four adjacent N/E/S/W crop squares.

## Farming: Farm Fence

- Farming, level 0, profession 1, makes Farm Fence
- Needs: 2x Copper Ore, 1x Iron Ore
- Unlock: Known at every crafting table. Gather the ore by mining.
- One farm square of metal fencing. Links north/east/south/west, including corners, T and cross junctions. Accepts a Fence Torch.

## Farming: Fence Torch

- Farming, level 0, profession 1, makes Fence Torch
- Needs: 1x Torch, 1x Copper Ore
- Unlock: Known at every crafting table. Gather the ore by mining.
- Mount on a Farm Fence to provide permanent warm light. Recovered with the fence when picked up.

## Farming: Grain Feed

- Farming, level 0, profession 1, makes Animal Feed
- Needs: 1x Wheat
- Unlock: Known at a crafting table.
- Craft homestead supplies from gathered materials and farm produce.
