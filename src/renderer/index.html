    Quests

    Picks up the quests you can do at your level from their NPCs, does them (kills, places to go, people to talk to) and hands them in for the rewards (needs the memory reader).

    Quests

    Hands in finished quests first, then picks up more, then works on the ones it has. Quests with tasks it can't do (fishing, mining, cards, instances) are left alone.

    It fights as Hunt does: attack, items and selling are set under ; spell keys and potions under .

    Boss circuit

    Takes the daily boss quests ticked below, goes round the sub-boss spawns they want (nearest and back first), kills them and hands the quests in for the Forge Stones; then, if you like, keeps going round for the bosses' drops (needs the memory reader).

    Quests

    Quests above your level are greyed out once the bot has seen your character.

    Going round

    Monsters further above your level than your fights say is safe (Grind's measurements) are skipped.

        Grind

        Levels your character up: picks the map with the most experience an hour for your level and class, travels there and hunts, moving on once you've outgrown it (needs the memory reader).

        Planning

        A new level, or leaving the map (a death, say), plans again straight away. How hard you hit and how far above your level is safe are measured as you fight, and the last plan says how far it goes.

        Selling

        Also when three items in a row won't pick up. Grind and Quests sell what Ludvik's Select All picks from the Main bag tab; upgrades and Legendary or rarer items are locked first and kept (see Stats & log).

        Quests

        Off, maps with quest monsters still get a bonus: they win when nearly as good as the best.

        Gather

        Walks to plants and ore on screen and gathers them, wandering when there are none (needs the memory reader and the tool in your Toolbelt). With Gathering trips, it goes where your profession levels gather best.

        What to gather

        Plants need a Scavenging Dagger and ore a Pick Axe in your Toolbelt.

        Trips

        The levels are read from the Professions window, which it opens and shuts once (Ctrl+Shift+P). Unticked, it gathers where you stand.

        Triple Triad

        Card matches at the card NPCs.

        Play

        Plays matches as you start them, picking the best move each turn. With the memory reader it reads every card's numbers and the rules in play from the game.

        Best deck

        Puts the best five cards you own into your deck. Open the card collection window first (needs the memory reader).

        Train

        Casts a spell on yourself over and over to train it, with the mouse resting on your character.

        Spell

        Keys & potions

        Hunt presses the ticked keys as it goes (F1 only while fighting), and so do Travel and Explore when they fight monsters in the way. Every mode but Triple Triad drinks the potions.

        Spell and item keys

        F1 is cast every round while fighting. Tick the others to press them too, at most once every so many seconds.
