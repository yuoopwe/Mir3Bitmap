    Card matches at the card NPCs.

    Play

    Plays matches as you start them, picking the best move each turn. With the memory reader it reads every card's numbers and the rules in play from the game.

    Best deck

    Puts the best five cards you own into your deck. Open the card collection window first (needs the memory reader).

    Train

    Casts a spell on yourself over and over to train it, with the mouse resting on your character.

    Spell

    Keys & potions

    Hunt presses the ticked keys as it goes (F1 only while fighting), and so do Travel and Explore when they fight monsters in the way. Every mode but Triple Triad drinks the potions.

    Spell and item keys

    F1 is cast every round while fighting. Tick the others to press them too, at most once every so many seconds.
